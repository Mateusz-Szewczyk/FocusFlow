A 4px dot and one sentence — used for what an assistant may and may never do, and for the caveats on a proposed plan.

```jsx
<BulletItem>Calls, Maps, bank and 2FA stay open throughout.</BulletItem>
<BulletItem tone="caution">Thursday runs three hours without a break.</BulletItem>
```

Amber is care, not alarm. Keep the sentence to one line of plain fact.
