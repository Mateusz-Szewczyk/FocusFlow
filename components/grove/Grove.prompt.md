Week, month or year of days rendered as a landscape of trees — the Grove tab, and the only "chart" in the product.

```jsx
<Grove view="month" />
```

- `view="week"` takes real data through `days`; month and year are deterministic sample stands.
- Pale days stay in. Never filter, sort or hide weak days — "a forest you edited would not be worth showing".
- Pair with one honest sentence underneath ("17 of 20 blocks."), never a dashboard of numbers.
